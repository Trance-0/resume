## Overview

Event planner who took ownership beyond assigned duties to keep a 300+ member WashU community active, informed, and responsive through graduation.

## Highlights

- Served as an event planner for a community of more than 300 WashU Chinese students interested in computer science and career development.
- Organized and participated in non-hackathon events covering LeetCode practice, interview skills, course selection, and academic planning.
- Supported a course helper program that assisted 15 students with schedule planning.

## Project narrative

### Situation

During my final two semesters, most club leaders were occupied with job searches, graduate-school applications, and their own projects, so the club was hosting fewer events and relying more heavily on online sessions.

### Task

Although the club website and media resources were outside my assigned responsibilities, I wanted the club to meet my baseline for being useful and responsive to its members through graduation.

### Action

I repeatedly checked the leadership group for missing opportunities, such as the narrow course-selection window, proposed events we could still run, helped plan and attended every club event except the hackathon, kept the website content current, requested post-event photos from teammates, listened to member feedback, and offered personalized help when needed.

### Result

Despite reduced leadership capacity, the club continued serving more than 300 members, and its course-helper program assisted 15 students with schedule planning.
