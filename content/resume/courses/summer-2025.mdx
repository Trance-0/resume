    description: |-
      The Freiwald Scholars program is directed to undergraduates with a proven record of strong mathematical skills who are motivated to carry out research in areas with substantial mathematical content. The program engages students in a reading and research project that lasts for at least two semesters and a funded (3K for 2026) 8 weeks in summer. 
       Abstract of research: To understand the following claim: In high dimensional subsystem of bipartite qbits, with high probability, a random selected state is almost maximally entangled. Topic of research skim through non-commutative probability theory (POVMs, partial trace, von Neumann entropy, tensor products), metric and measure spaces (L^2 spaces, complex projective spaces, Bargmann spaces), smooth manifolds (Riemannian manifolds, complex manifolds, kahler manifolds, compact lie groups), concentration of measure effect (levy family, high dimensional probability theory, maxwell-boltzmann distribution, invariant measures), representation theory (Majorana stellar representations, Weyl-Heisenberg group, symmetric products), quantum stochastic processes (coherent states), quantum information theory, and quantum computing. Who cares we start with Von Neumann algebra in the beginning?
